# Industrial Machinery Value-Chain Pilot: Source Log & Evidence Base

This document records the sources consulted for the industrial machinery
value-chain research preview (`data/pilots/industrial-machinery/brief.md`).

> **Evidence boundary:** Except for the stored trade dataset, the current links
> are mainly organization-level pages used to support directional E1 research.
> They do not by themselves verify the brief's detailed product specifications,
> capacity, quality, cost, imported-content, or feasibility claims. Those claims
> must remain hypotheses until linked to claim-specific publications or plant
> disclosures.
