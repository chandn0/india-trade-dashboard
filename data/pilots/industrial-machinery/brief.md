# Industrial Machinery Value-Chain Opportunity Pilot: E1 Research Preview

> **Evidence status:** This is a directional screening brief, not a feasibility
> study. The input relationships and capability statements are graded E1 because
> current citations are mainly organization-level pages rather than
> claim-specific production, capacity, quality, cost, or bill-of-material
> evidence. Monetary localisation impact is not estimated.

## Executive Summary

This pilot applies the domestic value-chain opportunity framework to India's industrial machinery sector across six priority HS-4 product families:
- **HS 8414**: Air/vacuum pumps, compressors and fans
- **HS 8421**: Filtering and purification machinery
- **HS 8428**: Lifting, handling, loading and unloading machinery
- **HS 8477**: Machinery for working rubber or plastics
- **HS 8480**: Moulds, mould bases and patterns
- **HS 8482**: Ball and roller bearings

Industrial machinery is a useful research area because India has adjacent
activity in structural steel, forging, machining, motors, gearboxes, valves,
and pumps. Those adjacent capabilities justify further investigation; they do
not establish that a particular imported machine can be produced competitively
or at the required specification and scale.

Across the six evaluated pilot headings, India recorded **$10.88B in gross imports**, **$4.48B in exports**, and a **net trade deficit of -$6.40B** in FY26.

## Detailed Product Family Findings

### 1. HS 8414: Air & Vacuum Pumps, Compressors and Fans
- **Trade Position**: Gross Imports: **$3,221.04M** | Exports: **$1,480.97M** | Net Deficit: **-$1,740.07M**.
- **Domestic Input Availability**:
  - **Electric Drive Motors (`8501`)**: `available` (CG Power, Siemens India, Bharat Bijlee, Kirloskar Electric). Standard IE2/IE3 induction motors manufactured locally.
  - **Structural Casing & Housings (`7208`)**: `available` (SAIL, Tata Steel). Heavy plate fabrication and standard fan housings fabricated locally.
  - **Rotational Bearings (`8482`)**: `limited` (SKF India, Timken, NBC). Standard ball/roller bearings available; high-speed turbocompressor angular contact bearings imported.
  - **Pressure Control Valves (`8481`)**: `available` (L&T Valves, Microfinish, KSB). Standard check and relief valves produced in India.
- **Missing Conversion Step**: High-pressure gas compressor housing precision casting, tight-tolerance screw/scroll rotor grinding, and flameproof/IE4 motor integration.
- **Research Priority**: **Near-term hypothesis** for standard industrial fans
  and compressors; high-pressure process-gas equipment requires product-level
  technology, specification, cost, and capacity evidence.

### 2. HS 8477: Rubber and Plastics Working Machinery
- **Trade Position**: Gross Imports: **$1,961.37M** | Exports: **$396.57M** | Net Deficit: **-$1,564.79M**.
- **Domestic Input Availability**:
  - **Extruder & Clamp Motors (`8501`)**: `available` (CG Power, Bharat Bijlee). Standard AC induction and hydraulic drives produced locally.
  - **Moulding Dies & Tooling (`8480`)**: `limited` (TAGMA India, MSME Tool Rooms). Medium-scale moulds manufactured locally; large automotive bumper and multi-cavity medical moulds imported.
  - **Machine Controllers & HMI (`8537`)**: `limited` (L&T Electrical, System Integrators). Panel assembly done locally; dedicated plastic machine PLC controllers (Beckhoff, KeBa) imported.
  - **Special Alloy Barrel & Screw Steel (`7218`)**: `limited` (Jindal Stainless, MIDHANI). Nitrided and bimetallic wear-resistant screw/barrel stock largely imported.
- **Missing Conversion Step**: Precision bimetallic barrel screw flight machining, high-torque AC servo drives for all-electric injection moulding, and integrated dedicated controller software.
- **Research Priority**: **Component-gap hypothesis**. Confirm the domestic
  assembly scope and imported high-precision subcomponents using plant or
  product-level disclosures before assigning a time horizon.

### 3. HS 8480: Moulding Boxes, Mould Bases and Moulds
- **Trade Position**: Gross Imports: **$1,137.74M** | Exports: **$240.32M** | Net Deficit: **-$897.42M**.
- **Domestic Input Availability**:
  - **CNC Machining & EDM Tooling (`8466`)**: `available` (Ace Micromatic, BFW, Jyoti CNC). CNC VMCs and EDMs widely operational in Indian toolroom hubs (Pune, Chennai, NCR).
  - **Mould Base Hardware (`8483`)**: `available` (Misumi India, Fibro India). Standard mould bases, guide pins, and ejector sleeves manufactured locally.
  - **Tool Steel Blocks (`7218`)**: `limited` (Alloy Steels Plant SAIL, MIDHANI). Pre-hardened ESR tool steel blocks (P20, H13, NAK80) largely imported.
- **Missing Conversion Step**: 5-axis high-speed surface milling, ultra-precision micro-EDM, and advanced hot-runner manifold system manufacturing.
- **Research Priority**: **Near-term hypothesis** for standard mould bases.
  Precision hot-runner, automotive, and medical tooling requires narrower
  product, tolerance, certification, cost, and capacity evidence.

### 4. HS 8428: Lifting, Handling, Loading and Unloading Machinery
- **Trade Position**: Gross Imports: **$1,123.68M** | Exports: **$365.57M** | Net Deficit: **-$758.11M**.
- **Domestic Input Availability**:
  - **Structural Frame Fabrication (`7208`)**: `available` (L&T Heavy Engineering, ACE, Elecon). Heavy structural steel fabrication and boom manufacturing extensively established.
  - **Hoisting & Travel Motors (`8501`)**: `available` (Bharat Bijlee, CG Power). Crane-duty induction motors manufactured domestically.
  - **Industrial Gearboxes (`8483`)**: `available` (Elecon, Shanthi Gears, Premium Transmission). Heavy helical and spur gearboxes produced locally.
  - **Control Panels & VFDs (`8537`)**: `available` (L&T, Siemens India). Crane control panel assembly done locally.
- **Missing Conversion Step**: Planetary winch drives for high-tonnage mobile cranes and imported high-performance VFD inverter modules.
- **Research Priority**: **Near-term hypothesis**. Verify the required crane and
  handling-equipment specifications, planetary-drive capability, certification,
  cost, and production scale.

### 5. HS 8482: Ball and Roller Bearings
- **Trade Position**: Gross Imports: **$1,568.72M** | Exports: **$813.23M** | Net Deficit: **-$755.50M**.
- **Domestic Input Availability**:
  - **Forged Ring Blanks & Balls (`7326`)**: `available` (Ramkrishna Forgings, Bharat Forge, SKF India, NBC Bearings). Ring forging and cold heading extensively operational.
  - **Bearing Grade Steel (`7218`)**: `available` (Sunflag Steel, Kalyani Steels). Clean SAE 52100 bearing steel produced domestically.
  - **Raceway Grinding & Superfinishing (`8466`)**: `limited` (Ace Micromatic). High-volume bearing assembly operational; high-precision raceway grinding and superfinishing CNC machinery imported.
- **Missing Conversion Step**: Ultra-clean vacuum-degassed steel for high-speed aviation bearings and CNC superfinishing raceway grinding machinery.
- **Research Priority**: **Component-gap hypothesis**. HS-4 trade values do not
  identify how much trade is standard versus high-precision bearings; that split
  requires HS-6/HS-8 and product-level evidence.

### 6. HS 8421: Filtering and Purification Machinery
- **Trade Position**: Gross Imports: **$1,872.45M** | Exports: **$1,186.94M** | Net Deficit: **-$685.50M**.
- **Domestic Input Availability**:
  - **Pressure Vessel & Housing Fabrication (`7326`)**: `available` (L&T, Godrej, Praj). ASME/PESO certified stainless steel vessel fabrication well established.
  - **Fluid Circulation Pumps (`8413`)**: `available` (Kirloskar Brothers, KSB India, WPIL). Industrial centrifugal pumps manufactured locally.
  - **Process Control Panels (`8537`)**: `available` (L&T Automation, System Integrators). Panel assembly and wiring established in India.
- **Missing Conversion Step**: Advanced synthetic membrane module chemical synthesis (RO/UF/NF membranes) and high-pressure ceramic filter element production.
- **Research Priority**: **Component-gap hypothesis**. Confirm the imported
  membrane and ceramic-element scope, domestic process capability, quality,
  cost, and scale before assigning a time horizon.

The ordering below identifies where to collect better evidence next. It is not a
commercial recommendation or implementation schedule.

   - **Net Deficit**: **-$758.11M** (Imports: $1.12B | Exports: $366M).
   - **Why investigate**: Several adjacent input capabilities are directionally

     mould-base activity. Narrower product, tolerance, hot-runner, cost, and
     capacity evidence is still required.

## Methodological & Evidence Disclosures

1. **Gross Imports vs Net Balance Interpretation**:
   - Evaluating gross imports alone is misleading. For example, HS 8414 has $3.22B in imports but also $1.48B in exports (46% export coverage). The true net deficit requiring localisation is -$1.74B.
   - HS 8482 has $1.57B in imports and $813M in exports (51.8% export coverage).
     HS-4 totals alone do not establish competitiveness or distinguish standard
     from high-precision bearings.

2. **No Double Counting of Component Deficits**:
   - Deficits of component headings (e.g. bearings `8482` or gearboxes `8483`) are kept strictly separate from finished machinery headings (`8414`, `8477`, `8428`) to prevent double counting.

3. **Suppression of Unsupported Monetary Claims**:
   - In strict accordance with the pilot methodology, monetary localisation impact remains explicitly suppressed (`monetaryImpactStatus = 'suppressed — missing sourced input and substitution shares'`).
